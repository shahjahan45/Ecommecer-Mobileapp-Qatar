# Phase 5 — Premium Product Details

This cumulative phase adds the complete DCX Online Store product-detail experience on top of all previous phases.

## Added
- Responsive product gallery with 3 visual states and thumbnail navigation
- Hero transition from product cards/list rows
- Wishlist toggle and share action
- Brand, stock, new-arrival and discount badges
- Product rating and review summary
- Variant/size/pack selector depending on category
- Quantity selector with stock-aware maximum
- Trust and fulfilment cards
- Product description and specifications
- Responsive customer-rating distribution
- Related products carousel
- Sticky SafeArea purchase bar with total, Add to cart and Buy now actions
- Mobile, tablet and landscape responsive layouts
- Product taps from Home and Product Listing now open the full details page
- Responsive widget tests for common phone/tablet sizes

## Architecture
The details page stays UI-first. No Laravel/API/authentication/database behavior was changed in this phase. Product information still comes from the existing demo catalogue until the API phase.
